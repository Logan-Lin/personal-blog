A few years ago, when I thought about low-effort [AI slop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_slop), especially that made during 2022 and 2023, when generative AI for images and natural languages had just caught the eyes of the general public, my main critique towards it was how low quality or inconsistent it was. Examples of this include [AI-generated language being hard to understand](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29162) and [obvious artifacts in AI-generated images](https://dl.acm.org/doi/full/10.1145/3706598.3713962). And many people probably think of it the same way, something that we just point and laugh at.

Fast forward to now, a (maybe uncomfortable) truth is that frontier AIs are improving in terms of most of the objective criteria that we can think of at an unprecedented speed. I actually firmly believe that frontier AIs being able to *objectively outperform* the majority of humans in any field is just a matter of time, if they haven't already.
But I will continue to have my critical eyes on AI slop, no matter how objectively good its quality is.

We humans are social creatures, and we are inherently [empathetic towards **other humans**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empathy). That means [valuing the **human effort** behind a product](https://culture.ghost.io/genai-is-our-polyester/) is one of our natural instincts, regardless of the objective quality of the final product.
Think about which of the following two scenarios will drive your empathy:
- A forklift (any forklift that was made in the past century) can lift a weight that no human can ever lift by hand no matter how hard they try
- Your friend who is relatively weak in muscles but, after months of training, is finally able to lift 40 pounds of weight in shoulder pressing

The nature of empathy is engraved into humans' DNA.
An example of this is that, while [hand gestures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_gestures) can have different meanings in different cultures since these are culturally assigned, the meaning of [facial expression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facial_expression) is largely universal since it is related to our capability of being empathetic.

So, until the human species can evolve to stop empathizing with humans, there will always be value in humanity, i.e., the human story, effort, and struggle behind a product, and that value cannot be replaced by AI slop, since AI slop, by definition, is a low-effort product.
I would even bravely predict that the value in humanity is only going to increase, as AI slop proceeds to flood our society.
There has never been a better time to be proud of making something with human effort despite the possibly naive product and low productivity, and to proudly show the human story behind your product, not just the final product.
